# Known limitations

- Meta credentials are not configured locally; no verified external engagement metrics are available.
- Meta Ads budget/campaign management and live publishing are not implemented.
- Website Deal publishing/removal requires an approved endpoint and token; local outbound configuration is absent.
- File handling stores validated private-storage metadata, not binary data; storage delivery/signing remains infrastructure-specific.
- Attribution coverage depends on valid tracking or authorized manual linkage.
- User-specific timezone preferences are not stored; the browser timezone controls display.
- No AI content generation, prediction, social listening, influencer management, email suite or ads manager exists.
- Internal UAT readiness does not imply production readiness or live CRM migration readiness.
