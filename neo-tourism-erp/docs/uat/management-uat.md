# Management UAT

Use UAT Manager. Management testing focuses on decisions, useful visibility, confidentiality, and workflow ownership—not data entry.

## M1 — Dashboard and queues

Review:

- Sales KPIs and lead pipeline
- Attention Leads and reassignment
- Sales Made / Operations queue
- Travel Soon and booking states
- Reconciliation pending and discrepancies
- Attendance and leave overview
- IT assets/tickets/access requests
- Notifications and Audit visibility allowed by the role

For each number, open its source module and compare a small sample. Confirm stale data becomes current after the expected refresh and that the same record is not counted twice.

## M2 — Permission and confidentiality review

1. Confirm Management can see information needed for oversight.
2. Confirm Management cannot perform specialist financial/HR/IT actions unless that is approved policy.
3. Check whether supplier cost, passport, employee, ticket, and financial details are unnecessarily visible.
4. Ask Sales, Operations, Accounts, HR, and IT to demonstrate one handover each.

## Questions for Management

- Which KPIs are useful or unnecessary?
- Which figures currently come from Excel or manual reports?
- What must be visible immediately after login?
- Which reports are needed daily, weekly, and monthly?
- Are targets, trends, comparisons, or date filters missing?
- Is confidential information visible unnecessarily?
- Are department handovers and ownership clear?
- Which limitation is acceptable for initial internal release, and which blocks work?
